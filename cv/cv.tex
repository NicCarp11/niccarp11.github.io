\cvsection{Research Interests}
My interests lie in \textbf{computer architecture and systems}, particularly \textbf{memory disaggregation} at rack scale and the scale-up interconnects, like CXL and related fabrics, that make it possible. Within that space, I focus on \textbf{cache coherence protocols} for heterogeneous systems.
